# Pilant — Product Video Script
**"One interface. 500+ apps."**

Format: live-action / motion-graphics hybrid, for a human editor or video production team to shoot/build from.
Length: 60–75 seconds
Tone: sleek, futuristic, confident — dark UI aesthetic, glowing connective lines, fast but readable cuts

---

## Visual style references

- Dark navy/near-black backgrounds (`#0B1220`–`#141C30` range), electric indigo/blue accent (`#6C7CFF`) — matches the actual product UI, so the video and the real app feel like one thing.
- Thin glowing lines connecting node-like app icons into a single hub — think air-traffic-control map crossed with a constellation.
- Clean sans-serif type (Sora/IBM Plex-style), monospace for technical accent labels ("REAL DATA", "LIVE").
- Screen recordings should be real product UI, not mockups — the actual inbox/interface footage, captured at 60fps, cursor movements smooth and deliberate (no frantic clicking).

---

## Script

### Scene 1 — The Problem (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** Rapid montage of a person switching between a dozen different app windows/tabs — email, Slack, a CRM, a support dashboard, a spreadsheet — each cut faster than the last, slightly chaotic, screens overlapping.

**VO:** "Your work doesn't live in one place. It lives in hundreds."

**On-screen text (final beat of the montage):** `500+ tools. 1 person.`

---

### Scene 2 — The Turn (0:10–0:18)
**Visual:** Hard cut to black. The chaos freezes and pulls back into small glowing dots scattered across the screen. A single dark hub pulses at the center.

**VO:** "What if you only needed one?"

**On-screen text:** `Introducing Pilant`

---

### Scene 3 — The Reveal (0:18–0:30)
**Visual:** The scattered dots animate inward, each resolving into a clean, minimal app icon, connecting via thin glowing lines into the central hub. Building into a single elegant network graphic — should read as premium, not busy.

**VO:** "Pilant is one interface for every app you use — email, support, sales, ops, and hundreds more — all speaking the same language."

**On-screen text (small, bottom third, appears/disappears quickly):** app category labels drifting past — *Email · CRM · Support · Analytics · HR · Finance · ...*

---

### Scene 4 — Real Product Demo (0:30–0:52)
**Visual:** Cut to real screen capture — the actual product UI. Show, in sequence:
1. A real inbox loading with real messages (2–3 sec)
2. Typing a plain-language request into the single search/ask box (2–3 sec, show the text appearing naturally)
3. Results appearing instantly below — real data, real subject lines (2–3 sec)
4. Clicking into one item, an AI-drafted response appearing in an editable box, cursor hovering over "Send" (3–4 sec) — pause just before the click to emphasize the human is still in control

**VO:** "Ask it what you need in plain language. Get real answers from real data — not guesses. And when Pilant suggests an action, you're always the one who confirms it."

**On-screen text (appears briefly under the Send button moment):** `Nothing happens without you.`

---

### Scene 5 — The Scale Statement (0:52–1:02)
**Visual:** Zoom out from the single interface back to the full network graphic from Scene 3, now fully built out — dozens of nodes, calmly pulsing, organized and orderly (contrast deliberately with the chaos of Scene 1).

**VO:** "500 plus apps. One interface. Built to be exactly as reliable as the work you trust it with."

**On-screen text:** `500+ apps. One interface.`

---

### Scene 6 — Close / CTA (1:02–1:10)
**Visual:** Cut to simple, clean end card — logo centered, hub graphic reduced to a small still icon beside the wordmark.

**VO:** "Pilant. Get started today."

**On-screen text:** `pilant.[yourdomain] — Get started`

---

## Music & pacing notes

- Scene 1 (chaos): slightly dissonant, quick tempo, low-key tension — should feel *mildly* stressful, not comedic.
- Hard tempo/key drop into Scene 2 — this is the emotional pivot point of the whole video, give it room (a half-second of near-silence before the beat drops works well).
- Scenes 3–5: steady, confident, mid-tempo electronic/ambient build — should feel like it's *resolving*, not hyping.
- Scene 6: music tails off into a single clean tone or soft fade, no dramatic sting — confidence, not a sales pitch.

## Alt CTA lines (swap Scene 6 VO if needed)
- "One interface. Every tool you already use."
- "Stop switching tabs. Start asking Pilant."
- "Your apps, finally speaking one language."

## Notes for the editor
- Every screen-capture beat in Scene 4 should be *real* product footage — this is a genuine differentiator (real fetched data, a real human-confirmed send step) worth protecting visually; don't let a motion-graphics mockup stand in for it.
- Keep total on-screen text sparse — the VO should carry the story; text is punctuation, not narration.
